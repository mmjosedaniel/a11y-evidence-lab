## Workflow-step technical assessments

The requirement IDs, wording, and statuses remain authoritative in [Evidence and review workflow requirements](../requirements/EVIDENCE_AND_REVIEW_WORKFLOW.md). These assessments are supporting Proposed architecture research. They cannot override a requirement or accepted ADR, promote an evaluation baseline, or authorize development.

The accepted MVP has a trusted public-page operating path and a controlled evaluation baseline. One `PageAnalysisRun` accepts one operator-entered public HTTPS URL under the documented trusted-operator boundary, loads it in a fresh non-persistent Playwright-managed Chromium context without imported user state, applies a finite navigation timeout, and performs one atomic axe-core scan with exactly `image-alt`, `label`, and `color-contrast` against the entire top-level document in its current rendered state at the configured readiness condition; iframe documents are excluded. OD-027 removes the prior authorized-and-trusted-use notice from the ready Analyze/Results presentation without weakening that operational boundary. A complete scan lists every returned violation node as an independently addressable `Finding` with nested minimized evidence and every native axe `incomplete` node separately as a `ScannerReviewObservation`; interrupted or invalid collection is another distinct condition, and zero Findings is valid only after all required native result arrays and exact per-rule coverage validate as complete. The user then selects one Finding at a time for retrieval and deterministic sufficiency. Incomplete required evidence or a completed `incomplete`, `missing`, or `conflicting` retrieval produces a terminal application-authored abstention with a clear explanation and manual-investigation guidance but no model call or proposal-review decision. A retrieval execution or integrity failure instead fails that FindingWorkflow with no support state. Complete required evidence plus a completed `supported` retrieval is eligible and may produce a validated proposal that alone enters human review. Comparison is a separate scan-evidence path that may start from any retained baseline Finding without prior retrieval, generation, abstention, or review. Scanner-review observations never enter either path, and no automatic all-findings workflow is created. Local-generation prompts and responses use only the approved Ollama loopback boundary, and embedding computation and vectors remain local in either generation mode. Trusted-page navigation remains external, while explicit Groq mode may separately send its accepted minimized generation payload; the MVP makes no system-wide zero-egress claim.

Exactly three independent, project-owned synthetic profiles in failing and corrected states remain the fixed evaluation baseline: `informative-image-alt` (axe `image-alt`, WCAG 2.2 SC 1.1.1), `form-input-label` (axe `label`, SC 4.1.2), and `text-contrast` (axe `color-contrast`, SC 1.4.3). Their fixture-specific assessment procedures support reproducible evaluation; they do not enter the user-submitted runtime URL path. Step 3 uses the bounded LangChain role accepted by [ADR-0013](decisions/ADR-0013-langchain-as-initial-rag-integration.md) for one selected finding. Step 4 uses the parent run's global `Local` or `Groq` mode and invokes that adapter at most once only for complete required evidence plus a completed `supported` retrieval. Incomplete required evidence or a completed `incomplete`, `missing`, or `conflicting` retrieval preserves the finding and produces abstention without a model call; a retrieval execution or integrity failure instead fails the FindingWorkflow with no support state. Review and comparison remain plain application-owned behavior. LangGraph, LangSmith, provider batches, and automatic fallback are outside the MVP.

The mapping basis is the official versioned [axe-core 4.13 rule descriptions](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core/blob/v4.13.0/doc/rule-descriptions.md) and normative WCAG 2.2 [SC 1.1.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#non-text-content), [SC 4.1.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#name-role-value), and [SC 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#contrast-minimum). A rule tag scopes evidence and retrieval; it does not make one automated result proof of complete success-criterion or page-level non-conformance.

OD-019 remains decision history for the three-profile evaluation baseline and its replacement of OD-002's earlier one-scenario scope. ADR-0018 accepts the single trusted operator URL boundary without promoting evaluation configurations to release dependencies. The local application service owns browser automation, provider calls, in-process exact vector retrieval, and one `run.json` aggregate; it does not own a vector-database service, model downloader, production egress proxy, or hostile-page sandbox. The MVP still includes no authenticated target scanning, user-profile reuse, intentional interaction, downloads, crawling, crawler implementation, automatic multi-finding orchestration, product-runtime agent, queue, workflow engine, installer, or desktop wrapper.

## Planned documentation areas

Add architecture documents only when the corresponding design has enough evidence to be useful. Expected areas include:

- System context and component boundaries.
- Runtime and deployment topology.
- Data flows, persistence, and lifecycle ownership.
- Trust boundaries, privacy, and security controls.
- Architecture diagrams and their supporting explanations.

Proposed architecture alternatives remain non-authoritative planning material until accepted. Significant durable decisions belong in an ADR and must be linked from the decision index; candidate documents must not be interpreted as changing the scope or status of an accepted ADR.
